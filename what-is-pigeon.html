A Small Bird with a Big Mission

01 / 05

Digital attention

The problem is not only false information. It is speed.

Core problem

Misinformation spreads in the moments before people think.

Current tools often react after harmful content has already moved through feeds and private chats. PIGEON focuses on the earlier moments: before posting and before forwarding.

01
The Creation Gap

Creators Create Under Pressure

  • Algorithms reward speed and engagement, not accuracy.
  • Creators lack simple tools to spot misinformation risks before posting.
  • The system favors viral content over responsible communication.
Speed over accuracy
02
The Media Literacy Gap

Consumers Engage Before They Evaluate

  • Users scroll past vast volumes of content daily, liking and sharing before critically evaluating what they see.
  • A single click can amplify information instantly, often outpacing fact-checking and thoughtful judgment.
  • The result is a persistent gap between rapid participation and the critical thinking needed to assess information responsibly.
Engagement before evaluation
03
The Trust Transparency Gap

Digital Trust Remains Invisible

  • Most content lacks clear trust signals: sources, evidence, and AI disclosure are often invisible.
  • Users can’t tell what’s credible, what’s synthetic, and what’s missing context.
  • Without transparency, informed engagement online becomes nearly impossible.
Invisible trust signals
04
The Opportunity Gap

Current Solutions Act After Problems Appear

  • Most solutions react after harm spreads: posts are removed, facts checked, and warnings issued too late.
  • Damage is already done by the time platforms and fact-checkers intervene.
  • Real protection requires acting at the source before harmful content goes viral.
Too late in the flow

Solution

A voluntary transparency layer, not a truth machine.

PIGEON does not block content or claim to decide absolute truth. It provides AI-assisted evidence prompts, credibility signals, and learning moments that help people make better choices.

Pre-publish
Step 01

Creator Assistant

Reviews drafts for source gaps, emotional language, claim clarity, and AI media disclosure before publication.

  • Source verification
  • Emotional tone alert
  • AI disclosure prompt
At share
Step 02

Pause Before Sharing

Presents short reflection prompts at the point of sharing, helping users add context or reconsider before a post travels further.

  • Reflective questions
  • Context suggestions
  • Micro-nudges
Ongoing
Step 03

Informed Choices

Makes credibility signals and Info-Grades easy to read, while keeping the original content accessible.

  • Five-dimension signals
  • Clear Info-Grades
  • No censorship or blocking
