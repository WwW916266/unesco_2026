Trust behavior

What improves a tier

Creators move up by making context, sources, and corrections visible before content spreads.

  • Link named sources before publishing claims.
  • Disclose AI-generated or edited media.
  • Add corrections when context changes.
  • Reduce panic wording in breaking-news posts.
