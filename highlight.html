What improves a tier
  • Link named sources before publishing claims.
  • Disclose AI-generated or edited media.
  • Add corrections when context changes.
  • Reduce panic wording in breaking-news posts.

Transparency label

Content gets context, not a pass/fail stamp.

The PIGEON label works like a nutrition label for information, showing transparency signals that users can understand quickly.
