Impact & SDG

! The core problem

Misinformation spreads in the moments before people think.

Current tools often react after harmful content has already moved through feeds and private chats. PIGEON focuses on the earlier moments: before posting and before forwarding.

01
Creator-side friction

Creators publish under pressure

Social platforms reward speed, emotion, and engagement more visibly than context, source transparency, or responsible framing.

Speed over accuracy
02
Consumer-side behavior

Users share reflexively

People often forward posts in emotional or trusted spaces before checking the source, missing context, or possible harm.

Reflexive sharing
03
Ecosystem gap

Trust signals are unclear

Digital content rarely shows whether claims are traceable, emotionally loaded, complete, or AI-generated.

Missing transparency

✦ The PIGEON solution

A voluntary transparency layer, not a truth machine.

PIGEON does not block content or claim to decide absolute truth. It provides AI-assisted evidence prompts, credibility signals, and learning moments that help people make better choices.

Pre-publish
Step 01

Creator Assistant

Reviews drafts for source gaps, emotional language, claim clarity, and AI media disclosure before publication.

  • Source verification
  • Emotional tone alert
  • AI disclosure prompt
At share
Step 02

Pause Before Sharing

Presents short reflection prompts at the point of sharing, helping users add context or reconsider before a post travels further.

  • Reflective questions
  • Context suggestions
  • Micro-nudges
Ongoing
Step 03

Informed Choices

Makes credibility signals and Info-Grades easy to read, while keeping the original content accessible.

  • Five-dimension signals
  • Clear Info-Grades
  • No censorship or blocking
